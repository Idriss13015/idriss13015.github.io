Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Version française

This policy explains which data the Pikacheck app (iOS and Android) and the app.pikacheck.com website use, why, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights. We never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Pikacheck is the controller of your data. For any question: support@pikacheck.com.

2. The data we use

2.1 Your account

  • Sign-in: email address and sign-in method (Apple, Google or email). With “Sign in with Apple”, the address may be a private relay provided by Apple.
  • Profile: your username (unique), your avatar (a character drawn in the app, not a photo of you) and, if you choose one, your favorite Pokémon.

2.2 Your collection

  • The cards and sealed products you add, their quantities, versions and purchase prices, your wishlist (and target prices) and your alerts.
  • If you create a share link for your collection, that page is public to anyone who has the link.

2.3 The social network (public content)

What you publish on the Pikacheck social network is visible to all members:

  • your posts and replies: text, photos, attached cards or products, polls, “Real or fake”, “Would you buy?” and “Openings” (product opened, price paid, cards pulled);
  • your likes, your votes (only shown as totals), who you follow and your follower count;
  • your username, avatar and Premium badge.

We also use, without making them public: the reports and blocks you make, and post views. Views are only counters: we do not keep who saw what.

Do not post personal information (address, phone number…): phone numbers, email addresses and messaging handles are automatically rejected.

2.4 Photos and camera

  • Card scanner: the camera image is analyzed only on your phone. No image is sent.
  • Posted photos: photos you add to a post are uploaded to our servers to be displayed. Before upload, an automatic check, also performed on your phone, verifies that they show a Pokémon card or product.
  • The app only accesses your photos and camera with your permission, and only when you choose to add some.

2.5 Community (listings)

If you share a Vinted or eBay listing, we store its link, title, price, public photo and the displayed seller name, to show it to other members.

2.6 Notifications

To send the notifications you chose (releases, Amazon invitations, replies and mentions on the social network…), we store your device’s notification identifier and your preferences. You can change them in Profile → Notifications. When you sign out, your device stops receiving your account’s notifications.

2.7 Premium subscription

Payment is handled by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We only receive the proof of purchase, to activate Premium. We never have access to your payment details.

2.8 Technical data and analytics

Device model, operating system, app version, technical identifiers and usage events (screens opened, features used), through Firebase Analytics, to understand usage and improve the app.

2.9 Advertising and measurement

  • Ads: free users see ads served by Google AdMob. In the European Union, your consent is requested at first launch; on iPhone, tracking permission (ATT) is also requested. Your device’s advertising identifier is only used with your consent.
  • Campaign measurement: on iPhone, the TikTok for Business SDK tells us whether a sign-up or subscription comes from our TikTok ads. The advertising identifier is only shared if you allowed tracking.
  • Amazon links: some links to Amazon are affiliate links. If you buy after clicking, Amazon pays us a commission; Amazon then knows you came from Pikacheck (its own privacy policy applies).

3. Why we use it

  • Running the service (performance of the contract): account, collection, prices, social network, requested notifications, Premium.
  • Safety and moderation (legitimate interest): automatic text filter (insults, private contact details, unauthorized links), photo checks, review of reports, removal of content and suspension of accounts that break the community rules.
  • Improving the app (legitimate interest): usage statistics.
  • Personalized ads and campaign measurement (consent, which you can withdraw).
  • Legal obligations: invoicing, requests from authorities.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the service providers needed to run the service, acting on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase: hosting, database, photo storage, sign-in, notifications, analytics. The database and server functions are hosted in Europe.
  • Typesense: catalog search engine (it receives your searches, not your identity).
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, in-app purchases, notification delivery.
  • Google AdMob and TikTok: advertising and measurement, as described above.

Links to Amazon, Vinted, eBay or other sites take you out of Pikacheck: their own policies apply. We may also disclose data when required by law.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers (Google, TikTok, Typesense) may process data outside the European Union, notably in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (European Commission standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and collection: as long as your account exists.
  • Posts, replies and photos: until you delete them, a moderator removes them or you delete your account.
  • Reports: as long as needed to handle them.
  • Usage statistics: 14 months at most.
  • Account deletion: from Profile → Delete my account. Your account, collection, posts, replies and photos are deleted; backup copies disappear within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to your data, its correction, deletion or portability, restrict its processing, and object to some processing. You can withdraw your consent at any time (ads: your phone’s tracking settings; notifications: Profile → Notifications).

Write to support@pikacheck.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, cnil.fr) or your local authority.

8. Minors

Pikacheck is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 15, ask a parent before posting on the social network. Content is filtered and moderated, and every member can report a post or block an account.

9. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), sign-in goes through Firebase Authentication, data access is restricted by server-side security rules, and only our team can access moderation tools.

10. Cookies (website)

The app.pikacheck.com website uses Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity to measure its audience. You can refuse or delete them in your browser settings.

11. Changes

Any change will be published on this page with a new update date. For significant changes, we will let you know in the app.

12. Contact

  • Email: support@pikacheck.com
  • Website: https://app.pikacheck.com
